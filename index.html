Ready for anything.

Raven Corps is a new group for adults who want real-world skills: map and compass, bushcraft, first aid, fitness, and being ready when things go wrong.

The meetups

First meetup: Saturday 23 January 2027

What
Shelter building and plant and fungi spotting in Delamere Forest, finding the way by map and compass. About two hours, finishing with The Roost: sitting on a log with a hipflask or a flask of something hot
When
1pm to 3pm
Where
Delamere Forest, near Frodsham. The meeting point is emailed to you when you sign up
Cost
Free for now, while we get started. Raven Corps will have a membership fee later on, and newcomers will always be able to try three meetups free first
Who
Over-18s. No experience needed
Bring
Boots, a waterproof, warm layers and gloves, your own food, and a hipflask of your favourite tipple or a flask of tea, coffee or hot chocolate. Driving? Make it the hot drink

What you'll learn

Skills that still work when the power doesn't

Map and compass

Read an OS map, take a bearing, give a six-figure grid reference and find your way when the phone dies.

Bushcraft

Shelters, knots, safe fire-lighting and working with natural materials.

First aid

What to do in the first few minutes, out on the hills and at home.

Fitness

Strength and stamina for real tasks: carrying, climbing and walking further with a load.

Ready at Home

Store water, purify it without chemicals, grow and forage food, then test it all in a yearly Ready Week.

Service

Help the local community and learn the history that shaped it.

For families

Sections for children open in 2027

Six sections take members from age 4 to adulthood, each with its own skills and ranks. Adults start first, so every section opens with trained leaders in place.

  1. Pioneers4–6
  2. Trackers6–8
  3. Outriders8–10½
  4. Lancers10½–14
  5. Commandos14–18
  6. Sabres18+Meeting from January

Register your interest through the sign-up form and tick the box for your child. We only ask for their age.

Get involved

Founding members wanted

Raven Corps is being built in Frodsham from the ground up, with chapters in other towns to follow. If you'd like to help shape it, or to start a chapter where you live, come to a meetup, or sign up and tick "Helping to run or instruct".

  • Ex-forces or cadet instructors
  • Outdoor, bushcraft and mountain leaders
  • First aiders
  • Teachers and youth workers
  • Organisers, treasurers and admin
