Get involved

Start a chapter in your town

Raven Corps starts in Frodsham. Chapters in other towns follow, led by local people, with everything they need from HQ.

In shortSign up and tell us your region. When about 15 people near you have joined, and at least one of them will help lead it, we'll help you start a chapter: the curriculum, the apps, the brand, training, and someone from HQ at your first meetup.

How a chapter starts

  1. People near you sign up. The sign-up form asks for your region and whether you'd help start a chapter. We keep a waiting list by region.
  2. A region gets ready. That means around 15 people nearby and at least one adult willing to lead, ideally two.
  3. We talk. A call with the people who'd lead it, to agree where to meet and when.
  4. Checks and training. References, a DBS check and safeguarding training for leaders, and a training day on running meetups the Raven Corps way.
  5. Find a place. Somewhere outdoors with the landowner's agreement, such as a forest, a farm or a country park.
  6. First meetup. Someone from HQ comes along to help it go well.

What a chapter needs

  • At least two adults willing to run it, so nobody carries it alone
  • Around 15 people interested nearby
  • A place to meet, with the landowner's agreement
  • Leaders who pass the checks: references, DBS, safeguarding training and first aid

What HQ gives every chapter

  • The curriculum: more than a hundred session plans across every skill and age group
  • The leader and member apps, for planning sessions, registers and signing off skills
  • The Raven Corps name, badge, uniform and brand kit
  • Risk assessment templates, a landowner letter and the safeguarding policy
  • Training for leaders, and a recommended insurance policy
  • Help on the day of your first meetup

How membership works

Meetups in Frodsham are free for now while we prove the format. In every chapter after that, newcomers can come to three meetups free before they pay a membership fee. Chapters run their own money and pay HQ a yearly fee per member.
