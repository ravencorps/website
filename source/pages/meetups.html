What a meetup looks like

Every meetup follows the same simple shape, so you always know what you're coming to.

  1. Meet and brief. Introductions, what we're doing today and how to stay safe.
  2. Learn a skill by doing it. Navigation, bushcraft, first aid, fitness or being ready for emergencies. Short explanations, then hands-on practice in small teams.
  3. The Roost. We finish sitting on a log with a drink we've brought ourselves: a hipflask of your favourite tipple, or a flask of tea, coffee or hot chocolate. It's where people get to know each other.

Places and skills change each month. Everyone who signs up gets the details by email the week before.

What it costs

Meetups are free for now, while we get Raven Corps going. Later on there will be a membership fee, and newcomers will always be able to come to three meetups free before they decide.

Staying safe

Every meetup has a risk assessment, and we only meet where the landowner has agreed. A leader carries a first aid kit and a phone, and we count everyone at the start and the end. We use only fallen wood, light no fires unless the landowner allows it, and leave every site as we found it. Everyone takes part at their own risk.
