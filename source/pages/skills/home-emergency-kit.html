What to put in a home emergency kit

Power cuts, floods, storms and burst water mains happen in Britain every year. A small stock of the right things means you can look after your household for a few days without help.

The short answerThe UK government's Be Ready advice is to keep enough supplies at home for three days. That means bottled water, food that keeps, a torch, a battery or wind-up radio, a charged power bank, some cash, copies of important documents, your medication, a first aid kit, and warm bedding.

The checklist

This follows the government's Be Ready checklist, grouped the same way.

Water

  • Bottled water for three days: about 3 litres per person per day for drinking, plus more for cooking and washing

Food

  • Food that keeps for three days, such as tinned meat, fish and vegetables, dried fruit and nuts. Choose things your household already eats

Light and warmth

  • A torch with spare batteries
  • Blankets, sleeping bags and warm clothes

News and contact

  • An FM radio, battery or wind-up, for official news
  • A charged power bank for your phone

Money and documents

  • Cash in coins and notes, as card machines may not work
  • Copies of ID and important documents
  • Important phone numbers, and a note of everyone's regular medication and doses

Health

  • A first aid kit
  • Essential medication and medical supplies

Babies, pets and hygiene

  • Baby formula (ready-to-feed is best), nappies and wipes
  • Pet food and water
  • Hand sanitiser, wet wipes and toiletries

Add a hand tin opener: it isn't on the government's list, but you'll want one for the tins.

Keeping it ready

  • Keep it together in one place everyone knows, ideally in a box or bag you can carry.
  • Check it every six months: swap food and water nearing their dates into your everyday cupboards, replace batteries and recharge the power bank.
  • Make a household plan: who you'd call, where you'd meet if you couldn't get home, and which neighbours might need a hand.
  • Practise: could you find the torch and the water in the dark? Try it.

Raven Corps members test themselves once a year in Ready Week, living from their own stores for a weekend or longer and noting what ran short.
