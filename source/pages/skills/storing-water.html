How to store water for an emergency

You can go weeks without food but only days without water, so in any emergency plan, water comes first.

The short answerKeep at least 3 litres of drinking water per person per day for three days, which is the UK government's Be Ready advice, and more for cooking and washing. Sealed bottled water is easiest. If you bottle your own, use clean food-grade containers, label them with the date, keep them cool and dark, and refresh them regularly.
