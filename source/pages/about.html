About

Ready for anything

Raven Corps teaches the skills that still work when the power doesn't, and builds people who are useful to their families and their community when things go wrong.

What Raven Corps is

Raven Corps is a community group that started in Frodsham, Cheshire, in 2026. It runs in local chapters, each led by local volunteers, with ranks, a uniform and a Code borrowed from the best of the cadet tradition. Unlike most youth organisations, it starts with adults: the Sabres section, for over-18s, meets first, so that every children's section opens with trained leaders already in place.

It isn't military and it isn't a religious group. It's for anyone who wants to be more capable outdoors and more ready for emergencies, and who likes doing that with other people.

What members learn

  • Map and compass: reading an OS map, grid references, bearings and finding your way when the phone dies.
  • Bushcraft: shelters, knots, water, safe fire-lighting, foraging and working with natural materials.
  • First aid: what to do in the first few minutes, outdoors and at home.
  • Fitness: strength and stamina for real tasks, built up gradually.
  • Ready at Home: storing water and food, growing and foraging, and a yearly Ready Week living from your own stores.
  • Service and history: helping the local community, and learning the history that shaped it.

Every skill has six stages, from first steps to instructor, so members always know what's next. Read some of it for free in the skills guides.

The Code

Faith
Live by the Christian values that shaped our country, and respect everyone's beliefs.
Loyalty
Stand by the King, your country, your chapter and your friends.
Courage
Do the right thing when it's hard, and keep going when you're tired.
Discipline
Be on time, ready and smart. Do your duty without being told twice.
Service
Put others first, and give your time to your community.
Honesty
Tell the truth, own your mistakes and keep your word.
Respect
Treat everyone as you'd want to be treated.

How it's run

Each chapter is its own constituted group, run by local volunteers under a chapter agreement. Raven Corps HQ, which looks after the curriculum, the apps, training and standards, is being set up as a not-for-profit company. Chapters pay HQ a yearly fee per member, so the organisation is funded by its members rather than by donations.

Safeguarding

Raven Corps has a written safeguarding policy and code of conduct. Anyone who works with under-18s needs two references, an enhanced DBS check and safeguarding training before they start, and there are always at least two adults. Each chapter has a named safeguarding lead. If you have a concern, email hq@ravencorps.co.uk.
